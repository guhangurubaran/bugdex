About BugDex 🌿

Welcome to BugDex! This app was created to help nature lovers, students, and curious minds identify, explore, and map the incredible world of insects right in their neighborhoods. By leveraging artificial intelligence alongside community-driven taxonomy, BugDex brings data like lifecycles, global distribution patterns, and safety details straight to your screen.
